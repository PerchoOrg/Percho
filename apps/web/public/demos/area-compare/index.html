Why this table marks a winner

The homes comparison deliberately does not — ranking whole houses on a composite would be our opinion dressed as a fact. Every row here is one measured quantity with an agreed direction, so marking the better cell states arithmetic. There is still no overall winner: how much schools weigh against cost is the buyer's judgement.
