Suwanee

City · Gwinnett County, Georgia

A railroad village of a few thousand people as late as 1990, Suwanee grew eightfold in thirty years. Instead of sprawling, it built a walkable town center in the 2000s and has kept adding parks to it. Most families come for the North Gwinnett schools.

History

A Shawnee village; the name is usually traced to the Creek word for Shawnee.
1871

What changes in the pipeline for a city film

  • Act 1 frames are generated from sourced data (chart, timeline, map) over the city's photos; the source line stays on screen.
  • One shot per place in Act 2, and never the same subject twice — City Hall appears once, as the title frame.
  • A shot must show the place itself: no animal, food or object close-ups standing in for a place.
  • Every narration line is about what is on screen, written from the research, not from Places review counts.
