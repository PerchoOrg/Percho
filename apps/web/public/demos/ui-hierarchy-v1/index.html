iOS visual hierarchy — before and after

Same screens, same content, same layout. What changed is which things read first: the three tiers of text, what amber is allowed to mean, and how fine print is set. The feed card, the feed header and the tab bar are untouched.

2 · Amber is a finding or a tapTile labels, event dates and the area percentage were amber too, so the figures they name came second. Labels are quiet now; amber is a rank, the community's own line, or something to press.
3 · A sentence is not a labelFootnotes and disclosures borrowed the section-label style: tracked capitals, in the faintest colour. They are 12pt sentence case now. What a figure needs to be understood moved up a tier.
4 · A card has an edgeWhite on the paper is 1.09 to 1. The You tab's cards were the only ones without the hairline every other card carries.
