Search lenses

The search tab keeps its map — a lens chip recolors it by one dimension, using each dimension's real geography (tax jurisdictions here; school attendance zones, utility territories in production). Nothing is filtered out: a lens changes what the map shows, not what exists — the §4.1 no-filter rule holds.

Tap a chip, then tap a county (map or ranking) for the breakdown. The True cost lens is the headline: what a $500k home actually costs per month, area by area — the buyer study's #1 post-move regret (hidden costs, 5/10 respondents) made visible before the visit.

Where each lens's data comes from

Schoolsalready in our DB — k12_schools + attendance zones + test scores
Property taxcounty/city millage sheets — public, free, a few hundred rows for the metro
Utilities & trashprovider territories + rate cards (Georgia Power vs EMCs), county sanitation pages — public
True cost /mocomputed: tax + utilities + trash + insurance; HOA joins at community level

Deliberately absent

No crime lens. Zillow and Redfin both declined crime layers on fair-housing grounds; we follow. The safety question is answered by resident reviews at community level instead.

Demo figures are approximate, county-level, for a $500k home. Production pulls the real millage/rate tables and goes finer-grained (city, school zone, subdivision).
