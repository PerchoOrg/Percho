How Percho works

A radical inversion of the real estate funnel. Instead of filtering listings by zip, buyers land on a neighborhood reel — 30 seconds of block, coffee shop, park, school — and 3-5 matching homes slide in at the end.

The core loop

Neighborhood-first, not listing-first.

Every reel opens on the neighborhood (3s hook), then community vibe (10s), then 3-5 listings quick-cut, then school/park close-out, then CTA. Buyers commit to the block first — conversion is 3× a raw listing reel.
